Part C

Opening sequence

15 seconds, landscape and vertical, each laid out for its frame. A page draws in, a scan turns its lines into tiles, the tiles leave the page and lock into a ledger, the grid folds into the emblem, and the name lands. Music and sound only, no words. It ends on solid Slate so an episode can cut straight in.

Rules

What stays fixed

RuleHow it is kept
Show nameAlways "Off Paper - Powered by Tokeniser". The text comes from one constant in src/brand.js, and check.py scans every source file for other spellings.
ColoursOnly Slate, Mint, Light Mint, Light Blue, Blue and Teal. Orange is left out of show art on purpose. check.py rejects any other hex or rgb value.
TypeMontserrat only, from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License. The identity PDF embeds no other font.
LogoThe three supplied PNG files, checked by SHA-256, only scaled evenly. Never recoloured or redrawn.
Devices and photosGraphic devices at 70 percent on solid colour and 60 percent on photos. Photos are grayscale with a 30 percent Slate overlay.
OpeningNo voice and no words. The only text drawn is the show name, and the music is synthesised in code, so there is nothing to license.
