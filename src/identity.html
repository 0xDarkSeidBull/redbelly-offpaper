Cut sheet. A page outline with the top right corner cut at 45 degrees. Also the shape of the thumbnail photo panel.

Ledger lines. Rounded bars that read as a printed page, never as words.

Token tiles. Squares on a strict grid. Light Mint while moving, Mint once they lock in.

Opening sequence, 15 seconds, landscape and vertical

0 to 2.5 s: a page draws in
2.7 s: a scan turns lines into tiles
4 to 7 s: tiles leave the page
7 to 9 s: grid links, verified in Mint
9 to 10 s: grid folds into the emblem
10 to 14 s: name and logo, then fade to Slate
9:16 layout

Motion is calm and exact: ease in and out, no bounce, no shake, one slow push in. Sound is original synthesised music: a D minor pad that resolves to D major as the name lands, with a fine tick for each line and a soft click for each locked row. No voice, no words, and the only on screen text is the show name and the Tokeniser logo.
